Current limitation
- The available Google Drive connector tools in this Codex session can import Docs, Sheets, and Slides files, and can search/list/read Drive metadata.
- A raw binary upload tool for ZIP/WAV/MP4 folders is not currently exposed.
- Google Drive desktop sync is not installed or mounted under ~/Library/CloudStorage.
- Therefore this inventory document can be uploaded through the connector, but the large binary media archives need either a raw Drive upload tool, Google Drive for Desktop, rclone/gdrive setup, or a browser-based manual upload flow.
