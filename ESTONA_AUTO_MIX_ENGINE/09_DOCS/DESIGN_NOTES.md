# DESIGN NOTES

- Stock/JSFX/Python-first, no paid plugin dependency.
- User-approved references define target ranges through abstract statistics only.
- Male lead vocal priority is the default.
- Female vocal defaults to backing/adlib support and should not become lead-like.
- Policies are conservative, normal, and aggressive, with normal as default.
- Every policy item must include a reason.
- Analysis does not equal final approval; listening checks remain mandatory.
