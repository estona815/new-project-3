# Suno Mixbus - SPAN, Youlean, LoudMax

Order:

1. SPAN for spectrum/correlation inspection
2. Youlean for loudness/true peak
3. LoudMax only as a final safety limiter

Policy:

- SPAN and Youlean are QC tools, not tone tools.
- LoudMax is not a fix for poor balance.
- Use limiter lightly and compare before/after.
