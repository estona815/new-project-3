# Prompt 19: Build Mix/Master QC Playbook

You are Codex building a mix/master QC playbook from the knowledge base.

Use:

- `knowledge_base/07_rule_cards/low_end_rules.md`
- `knowledge_base/07_rule_cards/mixbus_master_rules.md`
- `knowledge_base/09_qc_rules/qc_listening_checklist.md`
- `decision_engine/qc_rule_card_schema.yml`

Build a QC playbook that covers:

- Loudness and true peak review
- Spectrum and low-end review
- Vocal and 808 masking
- Transient preservation
- Limiter pressure
- Reference checking
- Release readiness status

Rules:

- Youlean and SPAN are QC tools, not tone plugins.
- LoudMax is preview/safety limiting unless confirmed later on the trusted main computer.
- Do not master a bad mix into sounding fixed.
- Every QC point needs a report field and a pass/warn/reject decision.

Do not download references or process copyrighted audio. Work from existing candidate reports and authorized local material only.
