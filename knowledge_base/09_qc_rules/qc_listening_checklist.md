# QC Listening Checklist

Use this checklist after each candidate render and before final selection.

## Vocal Repair

- Is noise reduced without vocal thinning?
- Is sibilance controlled?
- Is AI fizz less obvious?
- Are consonants intact?
- Is the vocal still emotionally close to the original?

## Mix Fit

- Does the vocal sit in the beat?
- Does the vocal feel pasted on top, buried, or washed out?
- Does the 808 mask the vocal or melody?
- Are hook phrases intelligible at normal listening volume?

## Master Safety

- Is the master loud without clipping?
- Are transients preserved?
- Is the low end powerful without causing pumping?
- Is the result release-ready, demo-ready, or regenerate-needed?

## Candidate Decision

Record one of:

- `release_ready`
- `demo_ready`
- `repair_more`
- `regenerate_needed`
