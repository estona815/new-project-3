# Low-End Rules

These rules target kick, bass, 808, and low-mid clarity in Suno repair and mix/master workflows.

## Rules

- Separate kick and 808 roles. Decide which element owns transient punch and which owns sustained sub weight.
- Check 40-80 Hz sub weight for power, translation risk, and limiter pressure.
- Check 100-250 Hz mud when the mix feels cloudy, boxy, or smaller after limiting.
- Avoid limiter-only loudness. If the limiter is doing the mix balance work, repair the mix first.
- Use sidechain or dynamic EQ only if needed. Do not add movement that makes the low end unstable.
- For digicore/hyperpop, keep the 808 aggressive but not masking melody or vocal intelligibility.

## REAPER Translation

- Use SPAN or spectrum QC to observe low-end buildup, but make final decisions by listening in full mix.
- Compare low-end candidates at matched loudness.
- Reject candidates that create pumping, vocal masking, or weak hook impact.
