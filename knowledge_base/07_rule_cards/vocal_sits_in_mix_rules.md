# Vocal Sits In Mix Rules

These rules convert vocal balance lessons into actionable mix decisions.

## Rules

- Vocal too loud is not always solved by volume down. First check masking, dynamics, and space.
- Check 2-5 kHz masking between vocal, synth leads, guitars, snares, and bright percussion.
- Use compression for stability, not loudness only. A stable vocal can often sit lower while staying intelligible.
- Use a short reverb or delay send to place the vocal in the mix when it feels pasted on top.
- Avoid washing out the vocal. If words move backward or consonants blur, reduce send level or shorten decay/feedback.
- Use reference checking to judge vocal level, brightness, and depth against a comparable release target.

## REAPER Translation

- Compare vocal bus trim, instrumental masking EQ, and light vocal compression as separate candidates.
- Use sends for space so the dry vocal remains controllable.
- Check vocal audibility in quiet and loud sections before final render.
