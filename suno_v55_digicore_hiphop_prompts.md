# Suno v5.5 Digicore / Hip-Hop Prompt Pack

Updated: 2026-05-05 KST

## 사용 전제

- 이 파일은 `SUNO_COMMERCIAL_DIGICORE_ARCHIVE.md`의 실행용 프롬프트 축약본이다.
- 업로드 오디오는 self-owned 또는 `suno_reference_pack/upload_allowed_4dium_i0i_only/` 후보만 사용한다.
- `suno_reference_pack/system_seoul/` 파일은 직접 업로드 금지, 참고 분석용이다.
- 특정 아티스트/유명인/타인 보컬 모방 지시는 금지한다.

## Final Master Prompt

```text
Korean sample-forward digicore / pluggnb / underground hip-hop, 146 BPM, F# minor family, 4/4. Build the track around an original dusty chopped memory-sample hook that appears within 15 seconds and repeats every 4 bars. Rough intimate male vocal in front, dry and emotional, commercial but raw; if an uploaded male vocal is used, preserve original rough male vocal character and do not replace the singer. Dry trap drums, half-time snare/clap feel, sparse hat rolls, warm saturated 808 with short slides, tight kick/808 separation, subtle bitcrush, tape hiss, clipped transients, small glitch fills at bar endings. Female vocals only as distant background ad-libs/harmonies/chops, never lead. No EDM drop, no festival build, no four-on-the-floor, no idol-pop gloss, no anime opening, no clean AI sheen, no artist imitation, no famous voice.
```

## Suggested Sliders


```text
EDM, festival build, festival drop, four-on-the-floor, big room, trance, house, future bass, dubstep wobble, supersaw, idol-pop, K-pop idol chorus, anime opening, clean AI sheen, robotic vocal, lead female singer, artist imitation, famous voice, vocal cloning, long intro, long buildup
```
