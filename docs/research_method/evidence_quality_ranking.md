# Evidence Quality Ranking

Use this ranking when assigning confidence to lesson cards and rule cards.

## A. Official DAW/Plugin Manuals

Official REAPER documentation and official plugin manuals. Best for exact feature behavior, meter meaning, routing, rendering, and plugin controls.

## B. Established Engineering Articles

Reputable engineering publications, vendor education articles, and clearly authored technical guides. Best for transferable concepts and decision logic.

## C. Experienced Mix Engineer Tutorials

Public tutorials by experienced engineers or educators. Best when the technique is demonstrated clearly and can become a measurable or listenable rule.

## D. Community Tips Confirmed By Multiple Sources

Forum or community guidance that appears consistently across reliable sources. Use cautiously and mark assumptions.

## E. Unverified Tips

One-off advice, trend videos, vague "secret sauce" claims, or untested parameter recipes. Do not automate unless later confirmed.
