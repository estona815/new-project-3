# Audio Engineering Notes

This system is tuned for digicore, hyperpop, emo hip-hop, melodic rap,
sample-flip, internet underground, and vocal-forward AI-assisted music.

## Working Assumptions

Suno/AI full mixes and stems are often already processed. They may contain
pitch correction, compression, limiting, EQ, stereo widening, reverb, source
separation residue, codec artifacts, and synthetic high-band texture.

The safest processing choice may be to skip a stage, request a better stem, or
route the work to human review.

## Priority Order

1. Lead vocal intelligibility
2. Vocal identity and emotion
3. Original timing and arrangement preservation
4. Mono compatibility and phase stability
5. Controlled high end and reduced artifact risk
6. Low-end translation
7. Loudness only after mix balance and premaster safety

## Common Measurements

- LUFS and short-term loudness
- true peak proxy
- RMS and crest factor
- LRA
- spectral tilt and band balance
- sibilance and harshness bands
- high fizz bands
- phase correlation
- side-channel energy
- low-end stereo risk
- vocal-to-instrument relationship

Measurements guide triage. They do not replace headphone review.

## Conservative Processing Bias

- Use dynamic EQ and sidechain carving before broad static cuts.
- Use send ducking before adding more reverb or delay.
- Use low-end control before master limiting.
- Use M/S side cleanup before stereo widening.
- Use pitch skip or micro-correction before strong tuning.
- Use artifact suppression before brightness or saturation.

## Claims

Do not claim reference matching, commercial master quality, listening approval,
or final delivery readiness unless the relevant measured reports and human
review records exist.
