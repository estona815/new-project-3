# Vocal-First Mixing Strategy

The system treats the lead vocal as the mix anchor. Vocal-forward does not mean
simply louder.

## Lead Vocal Goals

- clear consonants
- intelligible lyrics
- emotional proximity
- controlled sibilance
- stable center image
- preserved formants and identity
- enough texture to avoid sterile AI-clean tone

## Support Layer Policy

Female vocals, doubles, harmonies, and adlibs are support layers by default.
They should be tucked, panned, filtered, ducked, or muted when they compete with
the lead.

Support layers must not become the default lead unless the user explicitly asks
for that creative direction.

## Vocal Seat Carving

Prefer dynamic instrumental space over raw vocal gain:

- carve 200-500 Hz mud only when it masks the vocal;
- protect 2-5 kHz intelligibility;
- control 6-9 kHz sibilance and harshness;
- use vocal-keyed dynamic EQ on piano, guitar, synth, pad, sample, or rebeat
  buses;
- duck reverb and delay sends when the lead is active;
- preserve useful side width while cleaning side fizz or halo.

## Do Not

- fix buried vocal with only master limiting;
- add strong pitch correction before source safety checks;
- add heavy saturation to hide artifacts;
- widen phasey vocals blindly;
- push backing vocals into lead range without review.
