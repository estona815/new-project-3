# Candidate Decision Rules

- Do not choose the quietest candidate automatically.
- Reject candidates with watery or metallic artifacts.
- Reject candidates that damage consonants.
- Reject candidates that thin the vocal too much.
- Prefer dynamic EQ only if AI denoise damages the vocal.
- Prefer parallel blend if cleanup removes vocal body.
- Do not let LoudMax make a bad balance feel falsely better.
- Do not let reverb hide repair artifacts.
- Pitch correction candidates require explicit approval and are disabled by default.
- Final winner must be natural, commercially usable, and emotionally close to the original.
